In this unit, I brought everything from the course together: from a real problem to a working, tested solution that someone else can set up, use, and maintain.

## 🎯 Capability

**Solution Engineering**

The ability to turn a client problem into a complete solution: discovery, requirements, the right architecture, build, integration, testing, deployment, and handover.

---

## 🧠 What I Learned

- **Discovery & Requirements:** describe the current process, the users, functional and non-functional requirements, and what is out of scope.
- **Solution Selection:** choose between a workflow, an AI-powered workflow, RAG, or an agent, and justify why nothing more complex is needed.
- **Architecture First:** design the data flow and the failure points before building.
- **Integration:** prove that every component passes data correctly to the next.
- **Testing:** normal cases, missing data, invalid input, external failures, duplicates, and permissions.
- **Deployment & Handover:** where each part runs, how secrets are kept, how failures are detected, and how another person sets it up.

## 🛠️ Projects

### 01 — Final Project (Independent)

**Project:** Athar | أثر — Field work, turned into impact reports

- **Problem:** Women's organizations collect activity data by hand from messages and paper sheets, and the numbers in donor reports do not always match.
- **Solution:** Staff record activities on Telegram. At the end of the month, code calculates the figures, RAG retrieves the donor's requirements, AI writes the draft, and the manager approves it from an email.
- **Tools:** n8n, Telegram, Google Gemini, Google Sheets, Google Apps Script, Google Docs, Gmail

[Explore the Project](./Independent-Project/)

## 🔍 Engineering Focus

1. What real problem are we solving, and for whom?
2. Which parts need AI, and which parts must stay in code?
3. Why is this architecture enough, and why not something more complex?
4. What happens when a service fails or the input is wrong?
5. Can another person set up, run, and maintain the solution?

---

## 📌 Key Takeaway

**AI writes, code calculates, and a person approves.**

### 🧠 ماذا تعلمت

- **الاكتشاف والمتطلبات:** وصف العملية الحالية والمستخدمين والمتطلبات وما هو خارج النطاق.

- **المعمارية أولًا:** تصميم تدفق البيانات ونقاط الفشل قبل البناء.
